Euphoria is located in Kondapaneni Township, Mangalagiri, near Tenali Flyover. The project offers quick access to Amaravati, NH16, Vijayawada and other key destinations.

Euphoria is developed by R.G Constructions, with over four decades of engineering and execution experience in the real estate sector.

Euphoria offers premium 2 BHK and 3 BHK residences designed with spacious layouts and modern specifications.

The 2 BHK residences are 1,368 Sq.ft., while the 3 BHK residences are 1,939 Sq.ft. Both configurations are available in East- and West-facing options.

Euphoria comprises 116 premium private residences, designed with an emphasis on space, privacy and comfortable living.

Residents can enjoy a multipurpose hall, gym, table tennis, ample car parking, intercom facility, power backup, landscaping, round-the-clock security and solar power backup.

Euphoria is approximately 10 minutes from the AP Capital–Amaravati region, making it a well-connected residential address for people looking to live close to Amaravati.

The project is approximately 500 metres, or about 1 minute, from the NH16 Guntur–Vijayawada Expressway.

The 2 BHK residences start from ₹63 Lakhs and the 3 BHK residences start from ₹93 Lakhs. Contact our sales team for the latest pricing, availability and applicable charges.

Euphoria can be considered by both homebuyers looking for a well-connected residence near Amaravati and buyers evaluating property in the emerging Mangalagiri corridor. Speak with our sales team to understand the available residences and current opportunities.

Euphoria is approximately 20 minutes, or around 14 km, from Vijayawada Bus Stand and Railway Station.

Yes. Euphoria offers convenient access to leading universities and educational institutions including VIT, SRM, Amrita, KLU and ANU.
